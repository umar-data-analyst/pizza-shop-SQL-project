# Pizza Sales Data Analysis (SQL Project)

## Business Problem

A pizza shop generates a large amount of sales data every day, but raw data alone does not provide clear insights for decision-making. Business owners need to understand which products drive revenue, when customer demand peaks, and which categories perform best in order to improve operations and profitability.

Without analyzing sales data, it becomes difficult to answer important questions such as:

- Which pizzas generate the most revenue?
- What time of day has the highest number of orders?
- Which pizza categories are most popular among customers?
- Are a few products responsible for most of the overall sales?

This project analyzes pizza shop sales data using SQL to uncover patterns that can help the business make better strategic decisions.

---

## Project Objectives

The goal of this project is to use SQL to answer key business questions and extract meaningful insights from the dataset. The analysis is structured into three levels.

### Basic Analysis
Understanding the overall performance of the pizza shop:

- Total number of orders placed
- Total revenue generated from pizza sales
- Highest-priced pizza
- Most commonly ordered pizza size
- Top 5 most ordered pizza types

### Intermediate Analysis
Exploring deeper sales patterns using joins and aggregations:

- Total quantity of pizzas ordered by category
- Distribution of orders by hour of the day
- Category-wise pizza distribution
- Average number of pizzas ordered per day
- Top 3 pizza types generating the highest revenue

### Advanced Analysis
Generating strategic insights from the data:

- Percentage contribution of each pizza type to total revenue
- Cumulative revenue trends over time
- Top 3 revenue-generating pizzas within each category

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains transactional records from a pizza shop including information about:

- Orders
- Order details
- Pizzas
- Pizza categories


These tables were joined to perform comprehensive analysis of sales and product performance.

## Key Insights

- A small number of pizza types contribute a large portion of total revenue.
- Certain pizza sizes are ordered significantly more often than others.
- Order volume peaks during specific hours of the day.
- Some pizza categories outperform others in both quantity sold and revenue generated.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve business performance:

- Promote top-performing pizzas through targeted marketing.
- Prepare more staff and inventory during peak order hours.
- Introduce promotions for low-performing pizzas to increase sales.
- Focus inventory planning on the most popular pizza sizes and categories.
